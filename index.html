01 — THE IDEA

A digital experience
with a personal touch.

Birthday Verse is a birthday celebration website designed to make someone's special day feel memorable. It guides visitors through a delightful journey—from choosing a profile to discovering a birthday surprise and ending with a heartfelt thank-you.

The experience combines a dreamy cosmic aesthetic, personalized birthday messages, inspirational quotes, and celebratory animations into one interactive website.
